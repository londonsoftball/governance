## 1. Definitions
1. "the articles" means the Articles of Association of London Softball League Limited
2. "article" followed by a number means the article identified by that number in the articles
3. "The company" means London Softball League Limited
4. "directors" means a director of the company
5. "The committee" means the executive committee of the league, overseeing day-to-day operations
6. "officer" means a member of the committee who is not a director
7. "member" means an individual appointed by a team to be a member of the company
8. "2026 league rules" mean the Constitution and Standing Orders for the London Softball League unincorporated association, as they existed at the end of the 2026 League AGM

## 2. Authority

1. The London Softball League Executive Committee is created under article 6 to oversee the day-to-day operations of the company, including:
   1. Election of Directors and other Officers
   2. League registration and annual fees
   3. Standing orders governing play
   4. Code of Conduct
2. To ensure continuity in the league during the transition from an unincorporated association to a Company Limited By Guarantee, the committee will adopt all rules in the 2026 league rules
3. Per article 42, league rules must be consistent with the articles. Any existing league rule that is inconsistent with the articles is disapplied.
4. The committee will create revised editions of the league rules by the first AGM after incorporation, subject to approval by the directors.

## 3. Members of the LSL Committee

1. The committee shall include the directors holding the role of Chair, Secretary, and Treasurer.
2. The LSL committee shall consist of the following additional officers at a minimum :
   1. Technical Officer
   2. Fixtures and Results Officer
   3. Communications Officer
   4. General Officer representing a Majors team
   5. General Officer representing a Minors team
3. A full list of committee positions will be set out in the Standing Orders, with positions to be reviewed annually and added, amended or removed in response to the changing needs of the league. 
   1. The LSL committee may co-opt new officers to fill any non-director vacancies arising during the year.
      1. Any such appointments will terminate at the end of AGM following the appointment.
      2. Vacancies must be approved by two-thirds of the remaining committee members.
   2. No member of the LSL committee shall receive any remuneration from the LSL. Reasonable expenses incurred in the carrying out of LSL activities will be reimbursed on approval; evidence of the expenditure such as receipts etc. will be required.
   3. Any member of the LSL committee may also be a direct supplier or an officer, director, paid worker or employee of a company supplying a paid service to LSL, but must declare any conflict of interest and withdraw from any discussion and votes involving activities which they or their company have a financial interest in.
   4. Should an officer fail to attend and participate in committee meetings, or not perform the duties expected of them within their role, the committee may vote to remove them from the committee and make the post vacant. In the first instance the officer will be warned by the current League Chair. If the officer continues to miss meetings or fail in their duties then the League Chair can call a vote on whether to require the officer to step down and make the post vacant. Such a vote will require a two-thirds majority of committee members to pass.
   5. Should any officer be found to have seriously breached the LSL or BSF Code of Conduct (subject to the appeals process), a vote will be held at the next committee meeting on whether to remove them from their post. Such a vote will require a two-thirds majority of committee members to pass.

## 4. Election of Directors

1. The committee will organize the election of directors by members at the AGM
2. The Chair, Secretary, Treasurer, and two directors without portfolio form the board of directors (see article 17 of the Articles of Association)
3. Any person seeking election to these roles must be eligible under the Articles of Association (see articles 17 and 18 of the Articles of Association) and, if elected, must comply with all registration and verification procedures of Companies House.
4. The committee will notify members of the list of open director positions in advance of the AGM and request nominations, to be submitted in writing to the Secretary with a brief supporting statement.
5. The Secretary shall send all members a list of all nominations and statements not less than 7 days prior to AGM.
6. The election of directors shall be by paper ballot or electronic voting (as the committee deems appropriate) of all those present and entitled to vote.
7. In the case of any positions for which no nominations have been received in advance of the AGM, nominations may be received at the meeting itself and must be seconded.
8. Director nominees must be seconded at the AGM by company members
9. Director nominees can only stand for one position at any given time.

## 5. Election of Officers

1. The committee will organize the election of officers by members at the AGM
2. Officers of the committee are not company directors and shall not be registered as such. They shall have no vote on any matters reserved to the directors in the articles.
3. The majority of officers shall be individuals rostered to teams that participate in the LSL. Individuals who are not currently rostered to an LSL team but who have an interest in the league, and have relevant skills and experience, may be elected to committee posts with the exception of the following posts, which must be filled by current rostered players or team administrators: General Officer (majors or minors), Technical Officer.
4. The committee will notify members of the list of open officer positions in advance of the AGM and request nominations, to be submitted in writing to the Secretary with a brief supporting statement.
5. The Secretary shall send all members a list of all nominations and statements not less than 7 days prior to AGM.
6. The election of officers to the LSL committee shall be by paper ballot or electronic voting (as the committee deems appropriate) of all those present and entitled to vote.
7. In the case of any positions for which no nominations have been received in advance of the AGM, nominations may be received at the meeting itself and must be seconded.
8. Officer nominees must be seconded at the AGM by non-committee members
9. Officer nominees can only stand for one position at any given time. This does not prevent committee members from fulfilling the work of another committee role, should that role be vacant or additional help is required.
10. Any officer position may be filled by multiple people if those people have agreed in advance to work on that role together.
    1. If no agreement has been made, then they will be treated as separate candidates for that role.
    2. A joint role has one vote, and all holders of the joint role count as a single person for the purposes of establishing quorum.

## 6. Procedure of the LSL Committee

1. The committee shall meet monthly throughout the season, with additional meetings as needed.
2. The League Chair shall act as chair of the committee: in their absence, they shall nominate another director to chair the meeting.
3. The Secretary should give committee members at least 14 days’ notice of any meeting of the LSL committee; in an emergency the directors may call a meeting at 4 days’ notice.
4. The quorum at any meeting shall be one third or 6 of those members of the committee entitled to vote at a meeting, whichever is the smaller.
5. Committee meetings may be held in person, or using phone / video conferencing facilities, or a combination of these.
6. A record of attendance at committee meetings (in person or via phone / video) will be kept by the Secretary.
7. Each role of the LSL committee as listed in Section 3 shall be entitled to one vote. In the event of a jointly held role (as described in Section 5.10), all holders of the role must agree on their single vote or their role is considered to have abstained. Any matters arising shall be determined by a simple majority.
8. In the event of a tie, the chair has a casting vote.
9. Committee members may abstain from voting but no member shall have the power to veto.
10. The committee may use other forms of communication to vote on issues arising in between meetings, for example an online discussion forum. In such circumstances, the League Chair will state a timeframe for responses to the vote. At least 50% (rounded up) of the eligible members to vote must vote within the required timeframe or the vote does not pass.

## 7. Special and Standing Committees

1. The LSL committee may appoint subcommittees or working groups, with the approval of the board of directors. The proceedings of all such committees shall report to the committee. Sub-committees and working groups may include non-committee members but must be chaired by a current committee member.
